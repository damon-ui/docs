# Frequently Asked Questions

## General

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is FORGE?">
    FORGE is an AI-powered platform for travel advisors. It helps you create professional trip comparisons, build detailed itineraries, manage client relationships, and collect planning fees — all in one place.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How much does FORGE cost?">
    FORGE offers two plans:
    - **Starter** — $15/month for part-time advisors (up to 5 comparisons/month)
    - **Pro** — $29/month for unlimited comparisons and all features
    
    Early adopters get locked-in founding member pricing.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do my clients need to create an account?">
    No! Clients simply click the link you send them. They can view comparisons, vote, and see itineraries without any login or account.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## AI Builder

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What file types can I upload?">
    The AI Builder accepts:
    - Images (PNG, JPG, screenshots)
    - PDFs (booking confirmations, quotes)
    - You can also paste text directly
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How accurate is the AI extraction?">
    AI extraction is typically 90%+ accurate for well-formatted documents. Always review extracted details before saving — you can edit anything the AI gets wrong.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is my data secure?">
    Yes. Your uploaded files are processed securely and not stored long-term. Trip data is encrypted and stored in your private account.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Comparisons & Itineraries

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How many options can I include in a comparison?">
    Each comparison can include 2-3 trip options. This keeps decisions manageable for clients.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can clients see pricing?">
    Yes, by default pricing is visible. You can choose to hide pricing if preferred when creating a comparison.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I edit a comparison after sharing it?">
    Yes, you can edit comparisons at any time. Changes appear immediately when clients refresh the page.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Payments

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do payments work?">
    FORGE uses Stripe to process payments. You connect your own Stripe account, create payment links in FORGE, and funds go directly to your account.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What are the fees?">
    Stripe charges standard processing fees (typically 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction). FORGE does not charge additional fees on payments.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
