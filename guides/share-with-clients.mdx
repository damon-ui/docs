# Sharing with Clients

FORGE makes it easy to share professional trip presentations with your clients. No login required for them — just click and view.

## What You Can Share

| Content | Client Can Do |
|---------|---------------|
| **Comparison** | View options side-by-side, vote for favorite |
| **Itinerary** | View day-by-day trip details |
| **Payment Link** | Pay planning fees securely |

## Sharing Methods

### Copy Link

1. Open any comparison or itinerary
2. Click **Copy Link**
3. Paste into email, text, or messaging app

### Email from FORGE

1. Open the comparison or itinerary
2. Click **Email**
3. Enter client's email (or select from CRM)
4. Add a personal message
5. Click **Send**

<Info>
Emails are sent from your FORGE account with your branding.
</Info>

## What Clients See

When clients open your link, they see:

- **Your branding** (logo and tagline if configured)
- **Professional presentation** optimized for any device
- **Easy interaction** (voting, viewing details)
- **Your contact information**

<Tip>
Test your links by opening them in an incognito/private browser window to see exactly what clients see.
</Tip>

## Tracking Engagement

For comparisons:
- See when a client has voted
- View their selection and any comments

For itineraries:
- See when the link was last viewed (coming soon)

## Best Practices

<Tip>
**Personalize your message.** When emailing, add a brief note about why you chose these options for them.
</Tip>

<Tip>
**Follow up.** If a client hasn't voted in a few days, send a friendly reminder.
</Tip>
