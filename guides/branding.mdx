# Branding Your FORGE

Add your logo and tagline to make client-facing content look like it came from your agency.

## What Gets Branded

Your branding appears on:

- **Comparison pages** clients see when voting
- **Itinerary pages** shared with clients
- **Email footers** sent through FORGE

## Setting Up Your Branding

1. Go to **Settings** (gear icon in sidebar)
2. Click **Branding** tab
3. Upload your logo
4. Add your tagline (optional)
5. Click **Save**

## Logo Guidelines

For best results:

| Guideline | Recommendation |
|-----------|----------------|
| **Format** | PNG or JPG |
| **Size** | At least 200px wide |
| **Background** | Transparent PNG works best |
| **Orientation** | Horizontal logos work best |

<Tip>
If you have both light and dark versions of your logo, upload the one that works on white backgrounds.
</Tip>

## Tagline

Your tagline appears below your logo. Keep it short and memorable:

- "Your journey starts here"
- "Luxury travel, personal service"
- "Adventure awaits"

<Info>
Tagline is optional. If you don't set one, only your logo will appear.
</Info>

## Preview Your Branding

After saving:

1. Create a test comparison
2. Click **Copy Link**
3. Open the link to see how your branding looks

## Contact Information

Your contact details (from Settings > Profile) also appear on client-facing pages:

- Name
- Email
- Phone

Make sure these are up to date so clients can reach you easily.
