# AI Builder

The Builder is FORGE's AI-powered extraction tool. Upload screenshots, PDFs, or paste text — and let AI do the data entry for you.

## How It Works

1. **Upload** — Drag and drop images or PDFs into the Builder
2. **Extract** — AI reads the content and pulls out trip details
3. **Review** — Check the extracted information for accuracy
4. **Save** — Add the option to your library

## Supported Inputs

| Input Type | Examples |
|------------|----------|
| **Screenshots** | Cruise line quotes, hotel confirmations, supplier proposals |
| **PDFs** | Booking documents, invoices, itineraries |
| **Text** | Paste from emails or websites |

## Tips for Best Results

<Tip>
**Clear screenshots work best.** Make sure pricing, dates, and key details are visible and readable.
</Tip>

<Tip>
**One option per extraction.** Upload materials for a single trip option at a time for most accurate results.
</Tip>

<Warning>
AI extraction isn't perfect. Always review the extracted details before saving to your library.
</Warning>

## What Gets Extracted

The AI looks for and extracts:

- **Trip type** (cruise, resort, flight+hotel, etc.)
- **Dates** (departure, return)
- **Pricing** (per person, total, deposits)
- **Vendor/supplier** details
- **Cabin/room categories**
- **Inclusions and amenities**
- **Confirmation numbers**

## After Extraction

Once you've reviewed and saved an option, you can:

- Edit details in the **Options Library**
- Add it to a **Comparison** for your client
- Create an **Itinerary** from it
