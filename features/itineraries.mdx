# Itineraries

Create beautiful, detailed day-by-day itineraries for your clients. Perfect for complex trips, cruises, or multi-destination travel.

## Creating an Itinerary

**From a comparison winner:**
1. Open a comparison with a client vote
2. Click **Create Itinerary** on the winning option

**From the Options Library:**
1. Click the calendar icon on any option
2. FORGE creates an itinerary with dates pre-filled

**From scratch:**
1. Go to **Itinerary** section
2. Click **+ New**
3. Enter trip details manually

## Building Your Itinerary

Each itinerary is organized by days. For each day, you can add:

| Item Type | Examples |
|-----------|----------|
| **Flight** | Departure/arrival times, airline, flight number |
| **Hotel** | Check-in/out, room type, confirmation |
| **Cruise Port** | Port name, arrival/departure times |
| **Activity** | Tours, excursions, reservations |
| **Restaurant** | Dinner reservations, special meals |
| **Transfer** | Airport pickups, car services |
| **Note** | Tips, reminders, general info |

## AI Enrich

Use the **AI Enrich** button to:

- Upload booking confirmations and extract details
- Add items automatically to the correct days
- Fill in missing information

<Tip>
Upload cruise line confirmations to automatically populate port days with arrival/departure times.
</Tip>

## Sharing with Clients

- **Copy Link** — Share a read-only view with your client
- **Email** — Send directly from FORGE
- **PDF** — Download a printable version

## Client View

Clients see a clean, mobile-friendly itinerary with:

- Day-by-day timeline
- All trip details organized clearly
- Your branding and contact information
- Easy access on any device
