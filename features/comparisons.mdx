# Comparisons

Comparisons are FORGE's signature feature. Create a polished side-by-side view of trip options and let your clients vote for their favorite.

## Creating a Comparison

1. Go to **Options Library**
2. Select 2-3 options using the checkboxes
3. Click **Create Comparison**
4. Enter a title (e.g., "Johnson Family Hawaii Options")

## The Client View

When you share a comparison link, your client sees:

- **Side-by-side cards** showing each option
- **Key details** like price, dates, and inclusions
- **Vote buttons** to select their preferred option
- **Your branding** (logo and tagline if configured in Settings)

<Tip>
  Clients don't need to create an account. They simply click the link and vote.
</Tip>

## Sharing with Clients

From any comparison, you can:

- **Copy Link** — Get a shareable URL to send via email or text
- **Email Directly** — Send from FORGE with a customizable message

## Tracking Votes

When a client votes:

- You'll see a "Voted" badge on the comparison
- The detail view shows which option they chose
- Notes or comments from the client appear in the comparison

## Editing Comparisons

Click any comparison to:

- Update the title
- Add or remove options (up to 3)
- Change display settings
- View vote results

## After the Vote

Once your client votes, you can:

- Proceed with booking their preferred option
- Create an **Itinerary** from the winning option
- Request a **Payment** for your planning fee
