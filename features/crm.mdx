# CRM

FORGE CRM is built for **relationships, not pipelines**. Track client preferences, trip history, and important notes — not sales funnels.

## Adding Clients

**Manually:**
1. Go to **CRM** section
2. Click **+ New**
3. Enter client details

**Import from CSV:**
1. Click **Import**
2. Upload your CSV file
3. Map columns to FORGE fields
4. Review and confirm

<Info>
See [Import Clients](/guides/import-clients) for detailed CSV import instructions.
</Info>

## Client Information

For each client, you can track:

- **Contact details** — Name, email, phone
- **Tags** — VIP, family, honeymoon, etc.
- **Notes** — Preferences, important dates, trip history
- **Linked trips** — Comparisons and itineraries for this client

## The Power of Notes

Notes are the heart of FORGE CRM. Use them to remember:

- "Prefers oceanview cabins"
- "Anniversary is June 15"
- "Allergic to shellfish"
- "Loved the Alaska cruise last year"

<Tip>
When you use the AI Builder with a client selected, FORGE can pull relevant notes to help personalize suggestions.
</Tip>

## Linking Trips to Clients

When creating comparisons or itineraries, you can link them to a client. This:

- Shows the trip in the client's profile
- Helps you track their trip history
- Enables AI to provide personalized context

## Searching Clients

Use the search bar to find clients by:

- Name
- Email
- Tags
- Notes content
