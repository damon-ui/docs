# Contact Support

We're here to help! Reach out with questions, feedback, or issues.

## Email Support

The fastest way to get help:

<Card title="Email Us" icon="envelope" href="mailto:hello@forgehq.app">
  hello@forgehq.app
</Card>

We typically respond within 24 hours on business days.

## What to Include

When contacting support, please include:

- **Your account email** so we can find you
- **What you were trying to do**
- **What happened** (error messages, unexpected behavior)
- **Screenshots** if relevant

<Tip>
The more detail you provide, the faster we can help!
</Tip>

## Feedback & Feature Requests

Have an idea for FORGE? We'd love to hear it!

Use the **feedback button** (speech bubble icon) in the app, or email us directly.

## Report a Bug

Found something broken? Let us know:

1. Click the **feedback button** in FORGE
2. Describe what went wrong
3. Include the page/section where it happened

We take bugs seriously and will get back to you quickly.
