# Quick Start

Get up and running with FORGE in just a few minutes. By the end of this guide, you'll have created your first trip comparison ready to share with a client.

## Step 1: Upload Trip Screenshots

1. Click **Builder** in the left sidebar
2. Drag and drop screenshots of trip options (cruise quotes, hotel confirmations, etc.)
3. Click **Extract with AI**

<Info>
FORGE AI works best with screenshots that show pricing, dates, and trip details clearly.
</Info>

## Step 2: Review & Save Options

After AI extracts the details:

1. Review the extracted information
2. Make any corrections needed
3. Click **Save to Options Library**

Repeat for 2-3 trip options you want to compare.

## Step 3: Create a Comparison

1. Go to **Options Library**
2. Check the boxes next to 2-3 options
3. Click **Create Comparison**
4. Add a title (e.g., "Smith Family Caribbean Options")

## Step 4: Share with Your Client

1. Click **Copy Link** to get the shareable URL
2. Send to your client via email or text
3. They can view options side-by-side and vote for their favorite

<Check>
**That's it!** You've created a professional trip comparison in minutes instead of hours.
</Check>
